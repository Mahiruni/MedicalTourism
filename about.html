About TenaBridge

Ethiopian roots. Global care standards.

TenaBridge exists to make cross-border healthcare feel less fragmented, less opaque and more human—for people arriving in Ethiopia and for families travelling outward for advanced care.

Talk to our care team
Our mission

Be the trusted bridge between the patient and the next right step.

We are not a hospital and we do not replace the treating physician. Our role is to coordinate, clarify, translate, organize and stay present through the medical-travel journey.

Hospitality with discipline
