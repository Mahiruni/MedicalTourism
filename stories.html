Case study structure

What a responsible success story includes.

Context

Why travel was considered

Diagnosis, medical need, home-country options and what the patient was looking for.

Journey

What TenaBridge coordinated

Records, provider communication, travel, accommodation, language and companion needs.

Outcome

What can be stated accurately

Only outcomes the patient and provider permit TenaBridge to describe, with no guarantees generalized to others.
